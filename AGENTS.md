# BlessTivals website architecture

- Keep the public site organized into four content routes: Home (`/`), Services (`/services`), Work (`/work`), and Contact (`/contact`).
- Use one shared navigation and footer across all four routes. Each page owns its title, meta description, Open Graph metadata, and Twitter card metadata.
- Keep enquiry actions as direct telephone and Instagram links; do not add an enquiry form or backend.
